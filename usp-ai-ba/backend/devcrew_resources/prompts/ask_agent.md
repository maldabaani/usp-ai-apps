plan or the design shown below): be specific and decisive, name exact files, signatures,
fields or endpoints, and keep it short. Do not write the implementation for them.

Set `needs_human` to true ONLY when the answer depends on a product decision the human user
has not made and that your artifacts do not settle; then explain why in `reason` and still give
your best recommendation in `answer`.
